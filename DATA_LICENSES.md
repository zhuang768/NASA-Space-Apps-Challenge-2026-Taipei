# Data attribution and reuse

Original observations and research products remain attributable to their authors and providers.

- MOLA MEGDR: NASA MGS/PDS Geosciences Node; source labels and manifest retain product identity and observation periods.
- TES thermal inertia and interpolation mask: NASA MGS/PDS Geosciences Node. Thermal inertia is not temperature or hardness.
- SWIM 2.0: NASA-funded SWIM team, Planetary Science Institute; https://swim.psi.edu/SWIM2Products.php . Composite consistency is not ice abundance or probability.
- MCS time-adjusted temperature: Yu Wang and Shuanggen Jin, version DOI https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.17947556 ; CC-BY-4.0. Changes: nearest-coordinate extraction, MY29-36 selection, populated-bin and paired-bin summaries.
- Montabone dust scenarios MY34/MY35 v2.5: https://doi.org/10.1029/2019JE006111 and https://doi.org/10.1016/j.icarus.2014.12.034 ; CC-BY-SA-3.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/ . Dust-derived columns, series and other dust-derived outputs retain those terms. Changes: native-cell extraction, means/percentiles/maxima/seasonal summaries and exploratory trade-off selection. No warranty is provided.
- MEDA/PDS, https://doi.org/10.17189/1522849 : used as a rover-local availability/quality reference, not as measurements at these new targets.
- Published ice-exposing impact coordinates are cited to Morgan et al. (2025), https://doi.org/10.3847/PSJ/ad9b24 , Table 2. They are reference points, not new measurements.

Each source retains its own applicable terms. This notice does not assign a new license to third-party material or to the project's original code. A project-wide original-code license has not been newly granted in this cleanup.
