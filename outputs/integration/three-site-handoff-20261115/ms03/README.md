# MS-03 morphology observability checks

- Fixed cell: 57.50–58.00°N, 242.00–242.50°E, east-positive and planetocentric. Sphere radius 3,396.19 km. Computed cell area: **468.711 km²**.
- Five disjoint, preselected CTX review windows: **9.661 km² (2.061%)** by exact spherical rectangle areas from their retained KML bounding boxes. The five windows are not a whole-cell search.
- Three disjoint 512 × 512 MOC windows actually used in the prior visual review: **about 6.014–6.045 km² (1.283–1.290%)**. All 9,272,742 valid retained MOC pixel centres represent approximately **70.913–71.271 km² (15.129–15.206%)** of source support, not visual inspection. Range uses the min/max inverse area scale `((1+sin(lat))/2)^2` from the native polar stereographic projection and 3.000339463 m map spacing. This is a first-order valid-pixel area estimate without subpixel boundary clipping, and map spacing is not resolving power.
- CTX and MOC percentages must not be added without geographic overlap calculations. Pixels and bounds do not provide a calibrated polygon-detection threshold, nor a test of ice absence.
- **HiRISE catalogue check:** official [PDS ODE API](https://oderest.rsl.wustl.edu/live2/) valid map-projected product types RDRV11 (PDS3), RDR4 (PDS4) and DTM each returned zero products in the fixed cell. A pre-explicit 1.5° search box (57.0–58.5°N, 241.5–243.0°E) returned two RDRV11 products (COLOR and RED) for one observation, [PSP_006864_2380](https://www.uahirise.org/PSP_006864_2380), observed Jan 13, 2008. Both footprint polygons are west of the fixed cell. Its independent official [RED label](https://hirise.lpl.arizona.edu/PDS/RDR/PSP/ORB_006800_006899/PSP_006864_2380/PSP_006864_2380_RED.LBL) places the grid's eastern edge at 241.83751839088°E, west of the cell's 242.00°E edge. No claim that this is the closest image globally. RDR is a distinct non-map-projected family with only seven historical entries in the observed registry; its zero result was not used.
- The ODE registry separately reported 188,767 RDRV11, 16,518 RDR4 and 1,370 DTM products. The valid-product-family lookup and positive around-cell control reduce false-zero risk. Results remain bounded to these catalogue families and the server-reported snapshot time. The USGS parallel footprint endpoint returned 403, not a second zero result.
- **Disposition:** retain AD1 as a methods reference only. No verified Mars polygon boundaries, consistent geometry distribution, independent shallow-ice ground truth, actual ice depth, or ice fraction. The ~0.10 m conditional model depth in the research package is not measured. No change to or reinterpretation of the merged CRISM/SHARAD data.
- Three prior-work original tables (Cohen-Zada 2024, Levy 2009, Dundas 2021) remain missing. A new attempt on [Levy Data Set S1 PDF](https://agupubs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/suppl/10.1029/2008JE003273/supinfo/jgre2606-sup-0004-ds01.pdf) produced HTTP 403 HTML, not source rows. No original-table membership determination. Earth clock alignment remains outside this audit.
