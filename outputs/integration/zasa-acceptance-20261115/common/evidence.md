# Bounded independent ice-ground-truth check (2026-11-14)

Result: no exact-box independent ice-presence measurement established by this check. This is NOT evidence of ice absence. No remote changes or contacts. Existing MS03 Dundas S4/Cohen/Levy accessibility work was deliberately not repeated; no full exposure catalog spatial join is claimed.

- Direct surface ground truth: Phoenix at 68.219°N planetocentric, 234.249°E. Mellon et al. (2009), section 2, report robot-arm excavation and mechanical, spectral and chemical identification of water-ice/soil mixtures. This is genuinely independent of thermal/SWIM inference, but outside all three fixed boxes. Distances to box centers: MS01 3212 km, MS02 2002 km, MS03 654 km. It cannot authenticate local presence in any box. Primary paper: https://doi.org/10.1029/2009JE003417 . NASA's TEGA confirmation from a roughly 2-inch-deep trench: https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/news/nasa-spacecraft-confirms-martian-water-mission-extended/ . Coordinate cross-check: https://ode.rsl.wustl.edu/mars/pagehelp/Content/Missions_Instruments/Landing_Sites.htm .
- Seven independently reported exposure locations were read from Dundas et al. (2021) main text (Table 1 and Fig. 14), not S4. All fail box membership. These are a limited check, NOT the nearest-known exposure catalog. See observations.csv for coordinates and distances. Source: https://doi.org/10.1029/2020JE006617 . Table 1 records impact sites with icy coloration; its numerical layer depths are morphology-derived, not measured ice abundance. Fig. 14 identifies ice-exposing impacts in lineated valley fill, also far outside the boxes. The paper explicitly reports ice-table heterogeneity over tens to hundreds of meters, preventing extrapolation across hundreds of km.
- Viking 2 is outside the boxes, at approximately 47.668°N, 134.280°E in the PDS ODE coordinate list. Its observed seasonal surface frost is not proof of shallow buried ice in MS02 or another box. Do not silently substitute a Viking modeled ice-table depth for a lander measurement. NASA primary frost image: https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/imgcat/html/object_page/vl2_22e169.html . Older coordinate conventions differ across NASA pages; none affects the outside-box result.
