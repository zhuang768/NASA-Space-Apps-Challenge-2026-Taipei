# Novelty/challenge-fit disposition

Independent bounded primary-source check, 2026-11-15. No catalog rerun, organizer contact, submission, or repository modification.

## Decision
The four named locations are not defensible as newly discovered Earth analog sites. They can be shown as established analog benchmarks and cited prior work. A new pairing, implementation, or synthesis could be a project contribution, but is not equivalent to a new terrestrial analog, nor established as a world-first by this search. Novelty of any exact proposed workflow remains unproven until compared with original methods and outputs. No acceptance or eligibility guarantee follows.

## Exact 2026 challenge
https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/2026/challenges/permanent-moon-base-locations-and-mars/
Title: Identify Earth Locations that Analog the Permanent Moon Base Locations and Mars.
The indexed official page (crawled today) requires open Earth/Moon/Mars space-agency data, identification and characterization of new terrestrial analogs for future bases or landing sites, and a map, visualization or other tool. It explicitly names Haughton, Atacama and Death Valley as existing analogs, acknowledging incomplete characterization. Direct page open returned 502 twice; official indexed content was verified, but any additional detailed guidance not present there was not inspected. No verified rule says a new use of an existing site automatically substitutes for the new-analog objective. Do not import old-year FAQs as 2026 rules.

My interpretation: a provenance-rich comparison tool using known analogs is scientifically presentable and addresses part of the challenge, but the discovery/new-analog criterion is not yet demonstrated. Honest characterization should say so. A new application at a known site can be proposed as a candidate contribution with explicit prior-art contrast, not claimed as compliance guaranteed by NASA.

## Primary precedents
- University Valley: Marinova et al., Fifth Mars Polar Science Conference (2011), abstract 6051. https://www.lpi.usra.edu/meetings/polar2011/pdf/6051.pdf . Original describes 2009–11 field campaigns, year-round meteorology/subsurface temperatures and humidity, mapping ice depth, and using observations for thermal/vapor modeling and validation extended to Mars. Particularly p1 introduction and p2 Data collection/Modeling. Thus dry cover + shallow ice + thermal-model relevance is not a new site/use at this broad level. The 2013 paper also explicitly treats UV and Beacon as subsurface-Mars-ice analogs: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0032063313001360 (official indexed abstract read; full-page 403).
- Haughton: Nieto MSc 2005 original university thesis https://www.crewes.org/Documents/GraduateTheses/2005/Nieto-MSc-2005.pdf (indexed original text verified; direct download timed out) documents GPR/seismic subsurface geology and GPR ground-ice/water investigations explicitly as Moon/Mars H2O-rich analog exploration. A further 2010 original lunar GPR campaign is attested by author publication list https://troyunrau.ca/pubs and AGU abstract P23A-1619; do not rely on secondary reposts for precise details. Haughton is also expressly already known on the official challenge page.
- Beacon Valley: Nobes et al. 2018, original accepted manuscript https://pureadmin.qub.ac.uk/ws/files/158284264/Nobes2018.pdf , DOI https://doi.org/10.1109/ICGPR.2018.8441595 . Full text inspected. GPR profile interprets rocky permafrost ice, depth/lateral velocity variations and fractures; introduction expressly sets Mars-analog context and cites earlier ice/GPR work. A general claim that radar ice characterization at Beacon is new is contradicted.
- AD1: original Ulrich et al. 2011 https://doi.org/10.1016/j.geomorph.2011.07.002 and author dataset https://doi.pangaea.de/10.1594/PANGAEA.921087 . Dataset inspected, explicitly identifies Act-IWS as AD1 and states comparative high-resolution HRSC-AX/HiRISE terrain analysis plus field data and statistics with Utopia Planitia, Mars. Original article indexed abstract/site descriptions independently corroborate. Thus AD1 as Mars polygon analog and quantitative morphology comparison are prior art. This does not establish exact co-location with another thermal/radar dataset.

## What can be shown without misrepresentation
“An exploratory, reproducible comparison of selected Mars candidate regions with established terrestrial analog benchmarks, making data provenance, spatial resolution, missing validation and proposed future measurements explicit.”

For each alleged new use, show an existing-use/proposed-delta table: exact Earth footprint, target Mars footprint, tested physical process, inputs/resolution, algorithm, output, independent validation and prior closest study. Only label a delta implemented when demonstrable; hypotheses and proposed field tests remain proposals. A different Mars coordinate, new UI, re-indexed published model, or fused list of old datasets is not by itself evidence of discovery or validation. Never imply NASA endorsement, accepted submission, resource safety, landing suitability or world-first novelty.

Minimal gate for stronger novelty: identify a genuinely uncharacterized terrestrial location/use with bounded literature evidence; calculate transparent target-relevant comparisons; retain uncertainty and negative evidence; and demonstrate incremental information beyond the cited precedent. This is a research standard proposed here, not a NASA formal rule.
